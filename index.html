FAQ

Got Questions? We’ve Got Answers

Everything you need to know about our institutional material custody, container turnaround SLA, and ERP integration.

What types of capital cargo and industrial materials do you handle?

Thin Margin specializes in capital project cargo, including heavy transformers, solar modules, inverters, balance-of-plant (BoP) piping, structural steel, mechanized equipment, switchgear, and project-critical spare parts across thermal, renewable, and infrastructure sites.

How does your 24-hour seaport container decantation SLA work?

From the moment a container discharges at major maritime gateways like Mundra, Kandla, or Nhava Sheva, our ground team manages customs gate-out, unsealing, mechanized destuffing, visual inspection, and return of the empty container to the shipping line depot within 24 hours—guaranteeing 100% immunity from detention penalties.

How does Thin Margin integrate with enterprise ERP systems like SAP?

Our systems feature direct bi-directional integration with SAP MM, S/4HANA, and Oracle ERP. Inward physical inspections automatically generate Goods Receipt Notes (MIGO/GRN), while material requisitions by contractor crews trigger real-time stock deductions and audit trails.

What guarantees do you provide against material pilferage or damage?

We provide a contractual 100% Zero-Loss Guarantee. All laydown zones feature multi-tier security including RFID barcoding, 24/7 CCTV surveillance, biometric access gates, nitrogen purging maintenance for sensitive equipment, and full financial liability coverage.

Do you provide end-to-end statutory EHS and labor compliance?

Yes. All our personnel and mechanized rigging equipment adhere strictly to statutory safety standards (OSHA, ISO 45001). We maintain 100% compliance with central and state labor regulations, including Provident Fund (PF), Employee State Insurance (ESIC), and contract labor licenses.
