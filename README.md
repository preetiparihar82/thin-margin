# thin-margin

**The Thin Margin (TM)** - Precision Material Management, Warehouse Sizing & Turnkey Logistics Solutions.

Headquartered in Kota, Rajasthan, The Thin Margin (TM) delivers turnkey material management, 24-hour import container decantation, open laydown and temperature-controlled storage, and critical process implementation for India's leading infrastructure, power, and industrial projects.

## Core Capabilities
- **Expert Services**: Warehouse Sizing & Layouts, Critical Process Implementation, SAP/ERP System SLAs.
- **Value Added Services**: Material Requirement Planning (MRP, ROL & ROQ, ABC/VED analysis), MHE Optimization, Scrap/Waste Management.
- **EHS Commitment**: Zero-Harm to environment, ISO/QMS/EHS 9000, 14000 & 18000 compliance, 5S & Kaizen implementation.
- **Logistics & Haulage**: Over-Dimensional Cargo (ODC) handling, dedicated heavy fleet placement, and real-time GPS telemetry.

## Key Clients & Projects
- **Adani Group**: 1.2GW PV Solar Module plant (Mundra), 2 x 660MW Super Critical Power Plant (Kawai).
- **SB Energy (SoftBank Group)**: 648MW Solar Power Plant (Kamuthi, Tamil Nadu).
- **Aditya Birla Renewable Energy**: Solar module mounting and installation.
- **Ecom Express & Vishakha Group**: Supply chain and fulfillment operations.
